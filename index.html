Our Vetting Process

How We Verify Every Creator

Every creator on BookCreator.in goes through a manual review before their profile goes live. No bots, no inflated numbers, no fake accounts.

Sample Reel Review

We manually watch at least 3 recent reels to confirm content quality, local context, and genuine audience engagement.

Follower Authenticity Check

We cross-check follower counts against public analytics to flag sudden spikes or bot-inflated numbers before approval.

WhatsApp Confirmation

Every creator is contacted on the WhatsApp number they submitted to confirm identity and active availability before their listing goes live.

Local Kerala Focus

We only approve creators who actively cover local Kerala businesses. National or generic lifestyle accounts are not eligible.
