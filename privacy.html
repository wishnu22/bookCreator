Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026 • Last Updated: October 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to BookCreator.in ("we", "our", or "us"), a direct discovery directory for local content creators, food vloggers, and micro-influencers in Kerala, operated and maintained by BookCreator India. We are committed to transparency and respecting the privacy rights of all visitors, local businesses, and listed creators.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data footprint. We do not require account registration or passwords for browsing the directory. We collect only the following information:

  • Public Creator Data: Public handles, niche specialties, pricing tiers, sample portfolio links, and district locations provided either directly by creators via submission forms or compiled from public profiles with consent.
  • Submission Inquiries: Contact information (creator name, phone number, Instagram handle, commercial rates) submitted via the "List as Creator" or "Claim Profile" forms.
  • Local Browser Storage: Non-personal client-side data (e.g., shortlisted creator IDs, district filter preferences) stored locally in your browser for session continuity.

3. Direct Booking & WhatsApp Interactions

BookCreator.in operates on a 0% commission, direct-contact model. When a business clicks a creator's contact link (e.g., WhatsApp direct message), the interaction transfers directly to WhatsApp (a third-party platform operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.). We do not record, monitor, or intercept private messages, agreements, or commercial transactions exchanged between businesses and creators.

4. How We Use Information

Information collected is used strictly to:

  • Display accurate creator directory listings to prospective local business clients.
  • Process and review profile verification and claim requests.
  • Maintain platform reliability, prevent spam, and improve directory search functionality.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data or creator contact information to third-party ad brokers.

5. Profile Updates and Removal (Right to be Forgotten)

Creators maintain full autonomy over their directory presence. If you are a listed creator and wish to update your commercial rates, refresh portfolio links, or completely remove your profile from BookCreator.in, simply email us at support@bookcreator.in. Profile updates and deletions are processed within 24 to 48 business hours.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard HTTPS encryption across all platform endpoints. While no internet transmission is 100% immune from vulnerabilities, we maintain strict administrative access controls over our infrastructure.
